身體症狀嚴重程度在成人精神科門診患者中的預測因子：HRV與qEEG的作用

Clinical psychopharmacology and neuroscience : the official scientific journal of the Korean College of Neuropsychopharmacology · Predictors of Somatic Symptom Severity in Adult Psychiatric Outpatients: A Cross-sectional Heart Rate Variability and Quantitative Electroencephalography Study.

睡眠障礙是身體症狀嚴重程度的最強預測因子，其次為女性性別與憂鬱症狀；心率變異性降低與額葉-額額/額葉-額頂beta波同步性降低亦為獨立貢獻因子，ADHD診斷本身非顯著預測變數。
